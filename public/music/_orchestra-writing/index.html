Cinematic string (another way of saying epic) Link to heading

  1. four part writing
  2. Fifth voice is a filling line (no worry on parallel fifth) - add body to the piece
  3. Sixth voice for doubling bass line down a octave (can be divisi) (without filling)
  4. Seventh voice doubling soprano up an octave (divisi) (without filling)
  5. Eighth voice doubling top three voice up an octaves (divisi) (without filling)
  6. Nineth voice (1) Eighth voice with filler line in the middle (2) Copy the soprano down an octive to chello instrument.

How string vibrate Link to heading

  • First mode of vibration
    • regular play
    • Fundamential
    • pitch of the first mode of vibration eg. 98Hz for G
    • No division
  • Second mode of vibration
    • vibrating twice as fast e.g. 196Hz (one octave higher)
    • Division in the middle
  • Third mode of vibration
    • vibrating three time as fast e.g. 294Hz (D + 2 cents)
    • Division at 1/3 and 2/3 of the string
  • Forth mode of vibration
    • vibrating three time as fast e.g. 392Hz (D + 2 cents) three octave higher
    • Division at 1/4 and 2/4 and 3/4 of the string
  • There are infinite mode of vibration
  • The way string plays basically triggers the different mode of vibration for each of the notes.

Side Notes Link to heading

  • Wind instrument uses the same fingering system in order for the same wind player to be able to play different instrument. Because of that, the sounding pitch for every instrument are different. The sounding pitch of these instrument are called “Concert Pitch”
  • the Shape of the instrument defines their sounding
    • Cylindrical bore (cylindre like tube) -> Brighter
    • Conical bore (cone like tube) -> mellow Sound

Brass Instrument Link to heading

  • To be categorized as brass instrument: the sound needs to be produed by the lip vibration: Brass instrument = Labraphones
  • Without changing any valves, by modifying the mouth movement, the brass player can only plays harmonic series
  • Because the air is vibrating directly with the tube. The sound produced by brass instrument is mainly played on a Harmonic series.
  • Changing the length shift the Harmonic series.
  • Every single harmonic series has gaps where the pitches are missing, brass relies on changing the length of the tubes to produce different harmonic series via valves (Trumpet, horns, tuba, trombone for extending its slide). All of them combined together to form a complete chromatic scale
  • The longer the tube, the lower the note
  • Prefered to play in flat keys
