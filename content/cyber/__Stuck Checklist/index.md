# When Stuck on a problem

- [ ] Rule #1, **TRUST YOURSELF**
---
- [ ] Rule #2 **DON'T give any assumption**
---
- [ ] Rule #3 **List everything I have done** on a problem
---
- [ ] Rule #4 If a certain **problem is not solved due to a technical issue**, you have an idea but you do not see the result (given blank page or else), **try it again until a result is given**. (unless the blank page itself is the result). **Follow your thought of thread to the very end**.
---
- [ ] Rule #5 **Note down everything that is half done**, or else you will forget it.
---
- [ ] Rule #6 **Change** your **perspective**, or change the perspective of what you're working on.
---
- [ ] Rule #6 Always **double check** your **copy paste**, look out for the variable that hasn't yet changed.

---

# Side Tips
- When asking for a url, remember to **consider FULL url** including HTTP or HTTPS
- DO NOT assume if a word with or without spaces, just try both.
- Poison null byte: %00. Usefull to bypass name restriction. remember to encode it if using within URL format (%00 becomes %2500)
- XSS Testing `<iframe src="javascript:alert(`xss`)">`
