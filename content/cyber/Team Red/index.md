# Read Team Engagement

It complement penetration testing. It **tests the performance of the blue team** when a real attack happend. Mainly testing the detecting and responding techniques of the blue team.

The final objective is for the blue team to learn to react a real ongoing threat

Their engagement of emulating a real threat actor's Tactics, Techniques and Procedures (**TTPs**). 

Red Team **starts** by defining a **clear goal**: crown jewels or flags

### Read Team attack method involve:
**Technical Infrastructure**: uncover technical vulnerabilities with stealth and invation.
**Social Engineering**: phishing campain into revealing their information
**Physical Intrusion**: Using techniques like lockpicking, RFID cloning, exploiting weaknesses in electronic access control devices to access restricted areas
