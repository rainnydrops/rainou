# Extra Music resource
## Conduction
- Good resource for child music choir conduction: [Book for Building Child Choir: Lifeline for Children's Choir Directors](https://www.amazon.ca/Lifeline-Childrens-Directors-Ashworth-Bartle/dp/0769277012#:~:text=Lifeline%20for%20Children's%20Choir%20Directors%20offers%20extensive%20repertoire%20lists%20as,the%20field%20of%20children's%20choir.)
- It was written by the founding director of the Toronto Children's Chorus and includes repertoire lists for treble choirs and some very valuable tips and information
