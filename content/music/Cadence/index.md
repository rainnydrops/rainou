# Cadence

**Cadence is a musical punctuation:**
- it articulate phrase endings
- Everything that with V could be potentially be a cadence, if you feel the phrase actually ends here, then it is a phrase.

## Authentic Cadence: 
- End with V^(7)^-I

Give a sense of completeness
- **Perfect Authentic Cadence** (PAC): End with V-I **with tonic** in the melodic (primary melodic)
- **Imperfect Authentic Cadence** (IAC): End with V-I **without tonic** in the melodic

## Half Cadence: 
- anything ending with V
- Ending that is not finished
- Cannot use V7

**Reinterpreted half cadence**: It mostly occurs in the case of modulation or tonicization:  understood as the PAC or HC in another key

## Cadencial 64
- V^64^ - V^53^ see Chord Function

# Not really a Cadence
These are used to extends the phrasesk

## Deceptive Cadence:
- End with V^(7)^-vi
- Evaded cadence, phrases doesn't stops there.
- V, V7 Must in the root position
- It **tricks the listener into ending**, but it continues.
**Does not really fit an extension cadence**

## Plagal (Church) Cadence: 
- End with IV-I
- A-men Ending
- **An extension of the phrase**
- **Does not really fit an extension cadence**

## Phrygian Cadence: 
- In a minor key, End with iv^6^-V
- Ending at a climax
