- **X-7** (uses **Dorian** chord scale) (Because related II)

# SubV chord
SubV chord are the chord that can substitute dominant
- They have the **dominant quality** 
i.e. C7, A7, etc.
- Starts from **semitone above** the root chord with the same quality as C7, A7 is the subV of that chord. 
i.e. FMaj7 is the target chord, C7 is V/IV, G♭7 is the Substitution of C7
- **Chord scale for all of the SubV are Lydian♭7**
- Diatonic tension #9 can substitute for 9 in SubV/I and SubV/IV
- SubV's with diatonic tensions have minor target
- SubV's with only ONE diatonic tension have major target
